End-to-End (S2E) Doctoral Research Support

BookShita follows a structured Start-to-End (S2E) academic support model. Once engaged, the research scholar is supported throughout the complete doctoral journey, from initial conceptualisation to final thesis submission and award of the degree.

Support continues until the successful completion of the PhD, subject to agreed academic scope and institutional timelines.

Scope of Academic Support

  • Complete PhD thesis writing and structuring
  • Synopsis and research proposal preparation
  • Chapter-wise development and refinement
  • All university-mandated corrections and revisions
  • SDC, DRC, RAC and related academic review reports
  • Plagiarism compliance and referencing consistency
  • PowerPoint (PPT) preparation for pre-PhD, progress and final viva presentations
  • Research paper and research article writing
  • Peer-review level journal paper preparation
  • Guidance and support for journal publication process
  • Conference paper preparation and presentation material support

Note: Journal publication fees, conference registration charges, and travel or attendance expenses (if any) are not included and shall be borne directly by the scholar.

Deliverables & Conditions

  • All deliverables shall be provided in soft copy format only
  • No hard-bound or printed thesis copies are included
  • Final thesis soft copy will be released subject to completion of agreed payments
  • All revisions and corrections required by supervisors or committees are included
  • Work proceeds strictly in compliance with academic ethics and confidentiality
