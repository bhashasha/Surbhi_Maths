Who We Are

BookShita is an academic research and publishing support organisation dedicated to assisting PhD scholars, research students, faculty members, and academic authors across Indian universities. The organisation functions as a structured academic support system, providing assistance at every stage of thesis development, research writing, and scholarly book publication.

Academic integrity, confidentiality, and discipline-specific expertise remain central to every research engagement undertaken.

What We Do

  • PhD thesis structuring and academic writing support
  • Research proposal and synopsis development
  • Chapter-wise thesis organisation
  • Academic editing and language refinement
  • Referencing and citation formatting
  • Plagiarism reduction with academic integrity
  • Book writing, editing, printing, and publication support
  • Journal paper drafting and academic review assistance

Thesis writing and research support services are provided in both English and Hindi, in accordance with university norms and submission requirements.
