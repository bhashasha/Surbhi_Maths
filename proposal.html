Research Scholar Profile

This proposal is prepared exclusively for Ms. Surbhi Bansal, Research Scholar in Mathematics at Apex University, Jaipur. The objective is to outline mathematically rigorous and original research directions within the domain of Linear Programming, suitable for doctoral-level investigation.

Research Domain Overview

Linear Programming constitutes a fundamental area of mathematical optimisation, rooted in convex analysis, polyhedral geometry, matrix theory, and algorithmic mathematics. Despite its classical origins, linear programming continues to generate deep theoretical problems related to structure, complexity, stability, and geometry.

The proposed research directions emphasise formal definitions, theorem development, proof techniques, and analytical depth, without diversion into interdisciplinary or applied domains.

Proposed Core Research Themes

Structural and Polyhedral Analysis of Feasible Regions in High-Dimensional Linear Programming

This theme focuses on the geometric and combinatorial structure of feasible regions represented as convex polyhedra. Research may involve facial structures, extreme points, adjacency relations, degeneracy, dimensional complexity, and polyhedral decomposition. The study aims at establishing original results concerning vertex bounds, face lattices, and structural classifications.

Advanced Duality Theory and Stability Analysis in Linear Programming

This theme investigates the mathematical foundations of duality theory, including strong and weak duality, dual attainment, perturbation of constraint systems, and stability of optimal values. Emphasis is placed on deriving new sufficient conditions and proving structural properties of primal–dual relationships.

Degeneracy, Basis Structure, and Finite Convergence Properties of the Simplex Method

This theme is dedicated to the theoretical analysis of degeneracy and cycling phenomena in linear programming. Research directions include pivot rules, lexicographic ordering, finite termination proofs, and construction of pathological examples illustrating worst-case behaviour.

Algebraic Properties of Constraint Matrices and Integrality Conditions in Linear Programming

This theme examines matrix structures such as totally unimodular matrices, network matrices, and generalised matrix classes. The research aims to establish necessary and sufficient conditions for integrality of extreme points and develop new algebraic classifications relevant to linear programming theory.

Parametric Linear Programming and Sensitivity Behaviour of Optimal Solutions

This theme focuses on parametric variations in objective functions and constraints. The study involves piecewise linearity, continuity, breakpoint analysis, and stability regions of optimal bases, with the objective of proving new theoretical results on solution behaviour under perturbations.

Expected Mathematical Contributions

  • Development of original theorems supported by rigorous proofs
  • Formal characterisation of geometric and algebraic structures in linear programming
  • Analytical insight into convergence, stability, and complexity aspects
  • Construction of illustrative examples and counterexamples
  • Strengthening of theoretical foundations of linear programming
